Shipping & returns.

We keep things simple, clear, and easy to understand so you know exactly what to expect before, during, and after your order.

↗

Shipping

We ship to the address supplied at checkout. Once an order has been packed and dispatched, we’re unable to redirect it to a different address.

  • Orders are processed within a few business days.
  • Tracking details are sent once your order is on the way.
  • If an order is returned to sender because of an incorrect address, the customer is responsible for any additional shipping charges.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 15 days of delivery. Items may be gently used if they are still in like-new condition and suitable for resale. Refunds are issued back to the original payment method once the item is approved.

  • If an item is returned damaged, dented, heavily used, or not in acceptable condition, the return may be refused and the customer will still be responsible for the return shipping cost.
  • Items damaged in transit or not as described are reviewed before a refund is issued.
  • For any order issues, please contact us with your order details and photos so we can help.
